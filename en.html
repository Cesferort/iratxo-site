Build your world through focus.

How it works

What you do

You choose how long the expedition lasts and where the Iratxoak go. And you mark the apps that distract you: if you open one while they are out, they turn back and the expedition is lost. The rest is your own work; this is not a game you have to tend.

What they do

While you focus, the Iratxoak work. They come back with what the expedition gave, and they tell you what happened on the way. You watch the village grow: houses, ornaments and short games between expeditions. Never during one.

What stays on the phone

No account, no ads and no server. No opponent and no ranking. What you do stays on the device, and if you change phone you take your game with you, with a backup you make yourself in Settings. In Spanish, Basque and English.
